Charge sales tax at checkout with Avalara AvaTax, and keep your paid orders in AvaTax, ready for your reports and returns. Once you turn AvaTax on in Settings > Taxes, Swell asks AvaTax for the taxes on carts, orders and subscriptions instead of using its own tax rules.

Connect AvaTax with your account ID, license key and company code, and choose the sandbox or production environment. AvaTax works out the tax for each address, product and shipping charge, and paid orders are recorded in AvaTax automatically.

- **Tax at checkout.** Taxes are calculated once the customer enters an address, and again when the items, coupons, address or shipping charge change. Each item gets its own tax, and shipping is taxed too.
- **AvaTax tax codes.** Products use the tax code saved on them, and shipping and everything else use the defaults you choose. Gift cards get their own codes automatically.
- **Shipped from your locations.** Taxes use the ship-from address of your store locations and the taxable location you chose in Settings > Taxes, so origin-based rates come out right.
- **Sales recorded for filing.** Paid orders and invoices are saved in AvaTax as committed sales with the order number as the document code. Changes and returns update the document, and canceling the order voids it.
- **Tax-exempt customers.** Set an entity use code and exemption number on a customer's AvaTax tab, and AvaTax exempts their orders where the reason applies. Exemption certificates saved in Avalara under the customer's email apply too.
- **Your call when AvaTax fails.** By default, if AvaTax can't calculate the taxes, the cart or order change is rejected with an error. Turn this off to continue without tax instead.

Setup takes a few minutes. In AvaTax, find your account ID and create a license key under Settings > License and API keys, and copy your company code from Settings > Manage companies. Enter them in the app settings, save, and choose Actions > Test connection. Then turn AvaTax on in Settings > Taxes. If you use Swell's built-in Avalara AvaTax integration, turn it off so only the app records your sales.
