Charge the right sales tax at checkout with Avalara AvaTax, and keep every paid order in AvaTax, ready for your reports and returns. Once you turn AvaTax on in Settings → Taxes, Swell asks AvaTax for taxes on every cart, order, subscription and invoice instead of using its own tax rules.

Connect AvaTax with your account ID, license key and company code, and pick the sandbox or production environment. AvaTax works out the rates for each address, product and shipping service, and your orders are recorded in AvaTax automatically.

- **Tax at checkout.** Taxes are calculated as soon as the customer enters an address, and again when items, quantities, coupons or addresses change. Each item gets its own tax, and shipping is taxed too.
- **Products and shipping taxed correctly.** Use AvaTax tax codes on products and shipping services, with a default for anything that doesn't have one. Gift cards get their own codes automatically.
- **Shipped from your locations.** Taxes use the ship-from address of your store's shipping locations, so origin-based rates and use tax come out right.
- **Sales recorded for filing.** Paid orders and invoices are saved in AvaTax as committed sales with the order number. Changes and returns update the AvaTax document, and canceling the order voids it.
- **Tax-exempt customers.** Set an entity use code and exemption number on a customer's page, and AvaTax exempts their orders where it applies. Exemption certificates saved in Avalara for the customer's email apply too.
- **No untaxed orders by accident.** If AvaTax can't calculate taxes, the cart or order change is rejected with an error, so nothing is sold without tax. You can turn this off to continue without tax instead.

Setup takes a few minutes. In AvaTax, find your account ID and create a license key under Settings → License and API keys, and copy your company code from Settings → Manage companies. Enter them in the app settings, save, and choose Actions → Test connection. Then turn AvaTax on in Settings → Taxes.
